 A SMALL GUIDE TO GETTING LOST

Take the long way home.

  1. 01
    Wander the castle

    WASD moves your traveler; arrows turn and walk. Drag to orbit the camera. Tap M for routes.

  2. 02
    Follow the little lights

    Glowing envelopes hold letters. Desks and drawers let you leave one of your own.

  3. 03
    Let kindness become magic

    Open a new letter for +5 stardust. Leave a new letter for +20. Redeem it for a companion, shooting stars, and a secret room.

Progress and letters stay on this device during the playtest.

 THE WAY YOU WANDER

Find your rhythm.

Move with W A S D or the arrows. Hold Shift to run. Space to jump. Approach a glowing object and press E. Drag anywhere in the world to look around, or lock the mouse for free looking. Escape releases the mouse.

Your letters and rewards are saved on this device. The public letter service is not connected.
