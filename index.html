 A SMALL GUIDE TO GETTING LOST

Take the long way home.

  1. 01
    Wander the castle

    WASD moves your traveler; arrows turn and walk. Tap the movement arrows faster to run faster. Drag to orbit the camera. Choose any visible place card to walk there, or tap M for all routes.

  2. 02
    Follow the little lights

    Glowing envelopes hold letters. Desks and drawers let you leave one of your own.

  3. 03
    Let kindness become magic

    Open a new letter for +5 stardust. Leave a new letter for +20. Redeem it for a companion, shooting stars, and a secret room.

Progress and letters stay on this device during the playtest.

THE IDEA BEHIND MIDNIGHT CASTLE

A little wonder.
A little kindness.

A fantasy exploration game where a letter can be the most meaningful thing you discover.

Why this castle exists

Midnight Castle brings adventure and anonymous kindness into the same world. Its purpose is to give players a place to explore, find an encouraging thought, and leave a few kind words for whoever might need them.

01

Explore.

Wander four connected realms. Climb stairs, open drawers, and follow your curiosity.

02

Find a little light.

Discover a letter, read something unexpected, or write a message of your own.

03

Make kindness magic.

Earn stardust through letters and unlock a traveling lantern, shooting stars, and a secret terrace.

What makes it special?

The letters are at the heart of the adventure. Glowing objects, hidden corners, and small discoveries turn reading and writing into part of a world you can explore. Choose a traveler and a quest, or simply wander at your own pace.

Want an action adventure? In the optional Nightfall Defenders mode, protect letters from shadow enemies with a prism blaster. Read each rescued page to unravel a seven-chapter mystery and earn decorations for your own room. Peaceful exploration is always available.

Current playtest: letters and progress stay in this browser on this device. The letters you find include sample messages and your own saved writing; messages are not yet shared between players.

A LIGHT OF YOUR OWN

Your room awaits.

Walk inside your own room. Choose its colors, unlock furniture, and display the story pages you rescue.

Your mission: protect the glowing letter and your shield through three waves. Move with WASD or the arrows. Drag to look. Click a shadow to aim, or hold F / the FIRE button to auto-aim. R reloads; Space jumps. Read the rescued page to finish.
Combat earns embers for your room and blaster upgrade. Letter stardust and existing rewards stay separate. Progress saves on this device.
